Create a custom nonstandard threaded bolt-and-nut pair.

Required layout:
- Create both parts as one compound.
- Bolt axis is +Z, centered at (-14, 0).
- Nut axis is +Z, centered at (+14, 0).
- Both parts must stay within z in [0, 18].

Custom thread specification:
- Nonstandard right-handed, 2-start thread
- Nominal major diameter: 7.30 mm
- Approximate root diameter: 6.00 mm
- Pitch: 1.35 mm
- Lead: 2.70 mm
- Threaded engagement length: about 7.2 mm
- Use an asymmetric / buttress-like thread profile, not a symmetric ISO triangle

Bolt requirements:
- Threaded shaft centered at (-14, 0)
- Cylindrical head above the threaded section

Nut requirements:
- Matching internal thread centered at (+14, 0)
- Outer diameter about 13 mm and thickness about 8 mm

Scoring points:
- correct placement and sizes
- clear helical thread signal on both bolt and nut
- matching custom pitch / lead characteristics
- matching mating diameters and clearances
